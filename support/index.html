Spinned support

Need a hand with the show? Contact Spinned developer Raul Silva through the support tracker.

Send a support request

A GitHub account is needed to post. Requests are public: do not include passwords, Apple account details, email addresses, or other private information.

How to play

Hold your device in landscape. Tap the play card, then tap each dish to keep it spinning. Watch for wobbling dishes and reddening sticks. A falling dish costs you a ticket. Use the help button on the title screen for the controls.

Game Center

Game Center is optional. You can play without signing in. To check your sign-in, open your device’s Settings and find Game Center. Leaderboards require an internet connection.

Sound and interruptions

Spinned respects your device’s silent mode. Check silent mode and media volume if the game is quiet. When returning after an interruption, tap to resume if the pause message is shown.

Report a problem

Include your device model, iOS or iPadOS version, Spinned version, what happened, and the steps that reproduce it. If you attach a screenshot, crop out personal information first.

Privacy questions

Read the privacy policy. You can also use the support tracker to ask a general privacy question or request help removing information you posted.
