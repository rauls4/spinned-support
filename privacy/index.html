Privacy policy

Effective September 28, 2026 · Spinned by Raul Silva

This policy describes the Spinned iPhone and iPad app and its support website.

Information on your device

Spinned stores your best score on your device. The app does not send that local saved score to a developer-operated server. It does not include advertising, analytics, or tracking software, and does not request access to your contacts, photos, camera, microphone, or location.

Optional Apple Game Center

If you sign in to Apple Game Center, Spinned submits your game scores to Apple for leaderboard functionality. Your Game Center profile and scores may be visible to other players according to Apple’s services and your settings. Apple manages your Game Center account and related information under Apple’s privacy policy. You can play without signing in and manage Game Center in your device’s Settings.

This website and support requests

This website is hosted by GitHub Pages. We do not add analytics, advertising, tracking scripts, or cookies to these pages. GitHub may process technical information, such as IP addresses, to provide and secure its services. See GitHub’s privacy statement.

If you choose to contact us through GitHub Issues, your GitHub username and the content you post are public. We use that information to answer your request and troubleshoot the app. Do not post sensitive or private information. Support discussions may remain available to help other players; you can edit your own posts or ask for help removing them through the support tracker. GitHub may retain records under its own policies.

Your choices and deletion

Deleting Spinned from your device removes its locally stored score. Game Center records are managed separately by Apple. This app does not create a separate Spinned account. You can manage your GitHub posts and account through GitHub.

Children’s privacy

Spinned does not include a developer-operated registration system or intentionally request personal information from children. Apple Game Center and GitHub have their own account requirements and privacy policies. Children should ask a parent or guardian to contact support.

Changes and contact

We will update this page and its effective date when these practices change. For questions about this policy or information you posted, contact Raul Silva through the Spinned support page.
